Privacy Policy

Built On Hustle LLC. Last updated October 31, 2026.

Built On Hustle LLC runs the MyCupOfTeez shop on Etsy. Our Pin Publisher is a private tool used only by the shop owner to publish Pins of our own products to our own Pinterest account.

What the tool accesses

With the account owner's permission (Pinterest sign-in), the tool reads the owner's boards and Pin analytics and creates Pins on the owner's boards. It does not access any other Pinterest user's data.

How data is stored

Access tokens and Pin analytics are stored on a private server controlled by Built On Hustle LLC. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used to build advertising profiles.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under Apps. Stored tokens are then removed from our server.
